The two pathways are color-coded: purple for the genomic flow (unchanged), teal for the clinical covariate encoder, and brown for the merged prediction head.

Clinical covariates (cov_* columns in training_data.txt) are projected through a learned encoder Linear(clin_dim → h) → tanh → BatchNorm to match the root hidden embedding's dimensionality before concatenation. Both halves of the concat are h-dimensional, giving the final layer a (2h)-dimensional input.
